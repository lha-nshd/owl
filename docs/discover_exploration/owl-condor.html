OWL and Condor

How the two variable platforms relate to each other and how to use them together at each stage of your research.

OWL and Condor each approach NSHD variables from a different angle. OWL covers around 29,000 variables that have been cleaned and enhanced as part of the Metadata Enhancement Project and is the place to discover and understand them. Condor holds the full 35,000+ variable set and is where baskets are saved and shared ahead of a formal data request, which is made by completing the Data Access Request Form. OWL does not require registration.

How OWL and Condor work together

OWL and Condor cover different scopes and serve different purposes in the variable selection process. OWL is the richer discovery environment — it has questionnaires, topic documentation, additional searches, approved-project discovery and metadata that Condor does not. Condor holds the full variable set and is where baskets are finalised and saved ahead of a formal application.

OWL basket

Best for discovery and exploration. Use OWL to identify and refine your variable list using questionnaires, topic pages, and metadata — resources not available in Condor. Download your basket as a pre-filled Excel data dictionary to use as a reference list or to upload directly into Condor.

  • ~29,000 cleaned and enhanced variables
  • No registration required
  • Download a pre-filled Excel data dictionary for reference or Condor upload
  • Cannot be used directly for a data request

Condor basket

Where baskets are finalised, saved, and shared. Upload your OWL basket or build directly from the full 35,000+ variable set. Save and share your basket with colleagues before using its basket ID in your formal data application.

  • Full 35,000+ variable set including uncleaned variables
  • Registration required
  • Upload OWL basket and modify within Condor
  • Save and share baskets with colleagues
  • Data request made with the Data Access Request Form — not from Condor
The formal data request is made with the Data Access Request Form, not from within Condor. Once your Condor basket is finalised, complete the Data Access Request Form to make your application. A basket can contain more than 500 variables: Condor automatically splits larger baskets into baskets of 500 when you save. They are still treated as a single basket for your request, so a larger basket may take longer to turn around because of the risk assessment.
