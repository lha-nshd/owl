Approval is not the end of the process. Once your project is approved, you work with the data in the UCL Data Safe Haven, and there are separate steps for adding people, taking results out of the Safe Haven, checking papers before publication and extending your project. All of the forms mentioned here are on the Forms and Downloads page.

Receiving your data

NSHD data are released through UCL's Data Safe Haven, a secure environment. The Science Support Team deposits your data in a secure share and requests a Data Safe Haven account for you. Before you are given access, you complete the access prerequisites, and the Data Safe Haven team invites you to Information Governance induction training.

Not sure what to do next? Pick your situation in the diagram.

You then analyse the data inside the Safe Haven. Nothing is copied out to your own machine, and any results you want to take out are checked first (see Releasing outputs).

Adding users or more data

Everyone who uses NSHD data must be named on the project, return a signed confidentiality form, and hold a valid Data Security Awareness Training certificate. You will be told when each is needed. Use the Additional Users / Data Request form to:

  • add another user to your approved project. This can be done at any time, including after your data have been provided
  • request an additional basket of variables for your approved project
  • provide a basket ID if you did not include one in your original application, so the team knows what data you want to receive

Projects approved before 1 January 2023 should not use the form. Email mrclha.swiftinfo@ucl.ac.uk instead.

Releasing outputs

Results such as tables and figures cannot be taken out of the Data Safe Haven directly. To request them, complete the DSH Output Request form. Where appropriate, also complete the Pre-publication Checklist.

Before you publish

Every paper that uses NSHD data, including Insight 46 and MyoFit 46 data, is reviewed by the NSHD Data Sharing Committee before it is submitted to a journal. The review checks that confidentiality is protected and that the paper will not bring the study into disrepute, and the committee may also give you advice and feedback.

  1. Complete the Pre-publication Checklist.
  2. Email it to mrclha.swiftinfo@ucl.ac.uk.
  3. Wait for the committee's approval, then submit your paper to a journal.

Extending a project

If your approved project is due to expire, complete the Project Extension Request form and email it to mrclha.swiftinfo@ucl.ac.uk.

Send it in time. All extension requests must be received before the project's original end date has passed.

Citing NSHD datasets

NSHD datasets have DataCite DOIs, and authors must cite them in any publication that uses the data. The NSHD website gives the DOIs and how to cite them.
