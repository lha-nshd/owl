Access Prerequisites

The training, agreements and accounts you need in place before NSHD data can be released to you.

Four prerequisites must be completed before you can access NSHD data. You do not need to attach any of them to your application. After you submit your Data Access Request Form, an automated sequence of emails tells you what to provide at each stage, so you only need to submit the request first. The review process page shows where each step fits.

1. Data Security Awareness Training

New users

  • Complete the Data Security Awareness course and pass its assessment. It covers secure data management and the relevant current legislation.
  • The course is run on the e-LfH portal: Data Security Awareness course.
  • You need an e-LfH learning account, registered with your academic email address: register here.
  • Keep your completion certificate. You will be asked for it later.

Existing users

  • Renew the training every 12 months and upload your new certificate using the certificate upload form.
Applicants based outside the UK may have to pay a fee for the course. If this applies to you, include it in your grant budget.

2. UCL Data Safe Haven (DSH) access

NSHD data are released through the UCL Data Safe Haven, a secure environment, so everyone who will use the data needs a Data Safe Haven account. Two things are needed to set one up:

  • Approved Researcher Agreement (ARA). Sign and return the DSH ARA to mrclha.swiftinfo@ucl.ac.uk. You can download the DSH Acceptable Use Statement (PDF). UCL users can instead upload the statement directly on the UCL upload page.
  • Contact details. Send a mobile phone number or an alternative email address to mrclha.swiftinfo@ucl.ac.uk. These are used to create and maintain your Data Safe Haven account.
If you upload the statement directly, tell us. The NSHD team is not notified of direct uploads, so forward the confirmation to mrclha.swiftinfo@ucl.ac.uk.

3. Confidentiality Form

  • Required on your first application to the NSHD only.
  • It is provided after your request is approved. The principal applicant is emailed when it is due.
  • Forms from all applicants must be returned before the application can move forward.

You can read the form in advance: LHA Confidentiality Form v1.1 (PDF).

4. Data Sharing Agreement

  • Applies only to institutions that have not previously shared data with the NSHD. You will be contacted to provide it after approval.
  • It must be signed by an appropriate authority at your institution and returned to the LHA.
  • A new version was drawn up in October 2022 and updated in November 2025. All new projects must use the current version, which replaces all earlier ones. The agreement is non-negotiable.

Download the current version: LHA Data Sharing Agreement, 18 November 2025 (Word).

Key reminder. A Data Sharing Agreement must be in place between your institution and the LHA, and you must sign and return a Confidentiality Form, before any data extracts are provided.
