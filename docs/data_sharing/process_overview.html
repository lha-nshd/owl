From first look to receiving your data

Who does what, and in what order. Read left to right.

Text version of this diagram
  1. You explore variables on OWL, then build a basket in Condor and get a basket ID.
  2. You submit the Data Access Request Form with your basket ID.
  3. The Science Support Team reviews your request and basket.
  4. The Data Sharing Committee decides: approved, or more information needed. A decision takes 30 days at most, usually about two weeks. If you are not approved, you can appeal to the LHA Director.
  5. After approval, the Science Support Team emails you for the Data Sharing Agreement (if needed), the confidentiality form and your training certificate.
  6. You sign and return the forms and agreement and send your certificate.
  7. After approval, the Data Safe Haven team invites you to Information Governance induction training.
  8. After approval, the Science Support Team sets up your secure share and requests your Data Safe Haven account.
  9. You get access to your Data Safe Haven account and the data.

Which route do I use?

Most projects use Condor. Use UK LLC when you need NSHD linked to other studies or records.

What do I need to complete?

Answer three questions to see which prerequisites apply to you. You are asked for each one by email, so you do not attach them to your application.

Everyone: sign the Data Safe Haven Approved Researcher Agreement and send your contact details to the team.
Is this your first application to NSHD?
Yes
Every applicant returns a signed Confidentiality Form.
No
You do not need to send another one.
Has your institution shared data with NSHD before?
Yes
No new Data Sharing Agreement is needed.
No
Your institution signs the Data Sharing Agreement.
Are you new to NSHD, or is your training certificate more than 12 months old?
Yes
Complete or renew the Data Security Awareness course and keep the certificate.
No
Have your certificate ready to send when you are asked for it.

After approval: what do I do next?

Your project is approved and you are working in the Data Safe Haven. Pick the situation that matches.

Project approved
Need to add someone, or more data?
Additional Users / Data Request
Also use it to send a basket ID you missed. Projects approved before 1 January 2023: email the team.
Want tables or figures out of the Safe Haven?
DSH Output Request
Add the Pre-publication Checklist where it applies.
Written a paper using NSHD data?
Pre-publication Checklist
The committee reviews it first, then you submit to a journal.
Project about to end?
Project Extension Request
Send it before the original end date has passed.
Publishing?
Cite the dataset DOIs
NSHD datasets have DOIs, and authors must cite them.
