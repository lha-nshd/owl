Most NSHD forms are completed online and open in a new tab. A few are Word or PDF documents that you download, complete and email to the team at mrclha.swiftinfo@ucl.ac.uk. Only one form is needed to apply. The others are used later, once your project is approved. Read the access prerequisites first, and see how applications are reviewed for what happens after you submit.

Applying for data

Before you apply, choose your variables on OWL and build a basket in Condor, so you have a basket ID to include. If you are a student, your supervisor(s) must be named as co-applicant(s).

Guidance for the Data Access Request Form

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) on the form are mandatory. This guide follows the sections of the form.

1. Contacts

Contact details for the applicant and co-applicants. These are for internal use only, apart from the principal applicant's name, which appears on the list of approved projects. Institution details are needed because a data sharing agreement must be in place before data can be shared, and the co-applicant's institution is asked separately in case it differs. For further co-applicants, give name, position, institution and email. For PhD applications, name your supervisor(s) as co-applicant(s). You can submit without a final team and add users later with the Additional Users / Data Request form.

2. Project information

Approved projects are published for transparency, and applicants can check whether similar work has already been approved. This information appears on the public list of approved projects, so submitting the form means you consent to this limited disclosure. If you do not consent, do not submit.

3. Funding and description

Internal use only. It is used to check whether the proposal has already been peer reviewed and whether you can carry out the work. Give a brief description including proposed outputs, in no more than one side of A4 or 500 words, with no more than 10 key references.

4. Public summary

Shown publicly alongside the project name and dates from section 2. Maximum 150 words, with no references.

5. Specialist datasets

Internal use only. It confirms that the team has the experience and expertise to analyse specialist data.

6. Imaging data

Internal use only. State whether the project involves Insight 46 or imaging data, and if so which sequences you need. Imaging datasets are complex, so show that the team can analyse them, including feasibility and quality control.

7. Variables required

Internal use only. Include your list of NSHD variable names if you have one (your OWL or Condor basket gives you this). If not, describe the variables you need and the years or data collections they come from. The team checks for variables that could be disclosive or produce low cell counts.

8. How you heard

Internal use only. It records how applicants heard about NSHD data, for internal metrics.
